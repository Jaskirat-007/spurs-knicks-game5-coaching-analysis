# Analysis Output Guide

The build exports CSV files to `data/processed/analysis_outputs/`.

- `team_comparison_windows.csv`: Regular season, pre-Finals playoffs, Finals Games 1-4, and last-10 team identity comparison.
- `finals_games_1_4_team_stats.csv`: Game-by-game Four Factors and ratings for the first four Finals games.
- `player_comparison_windows.csv`: Player production and advanced usage across the four comparison windows.
- `pregame_matchup_player_games.csv`: Player-level results from the two regular-season meetings, NBA Cup Final, and Finals Games 1-4.
- `pregame_shot_profiles.csv`: Shot frequency and efficiency by location and matchup sample.
- `pregame_lineup_analysis.csv`: Meaningful five-player lineup results by analysis window.
- `finals_quarter_summary.csv`: Points, turnovers, fouls, timeouts, and substitutions by quarter in Games 1-4.
- `finals_scoring_runs.csv`: Consecutive scoring runs of at least six points.
- `finals_rotation_events.csv`: Substitution and timeout timeline for Games 1-4.

Use the pregame files to form and freeze recommendations before opening any holdout output.
